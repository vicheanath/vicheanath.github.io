---

There is something to be said for reading when the house is quiet and the hour is late. The world outside has settled; the only sound is the turn of a page.

## A habit of attention

We are told to read more. Perhaps we might read *better*: with less hurry, with a pencil in hand, with the willingness to pause and look up from the book.

## What we keep

Not every sentence will stay with us. But the ones that do — the line that returns unbidden months later — those are the ones that change how we think. They deserve a little of our time.
